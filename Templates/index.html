Penalty points system

In case you didn't deposit your package within 30 minutes after the reservation, a penalty will be applied following these rules:

  • First penalty: A warn message is sent.
  • Second penalty: +1 penalty point and you can't reserve any locker during 24 hours.
  • Next penalties Any other penalty after the first will apply an additional +1 penalty point and a prohibition from the locker reservation system lasting for a duration calculated with the number of points accumulated (1 points = +24 hours)

Reset conditions: The penalty points last for either 7 reservations on time or 30 days.
