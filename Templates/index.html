Rules and penalty score

This app allows you to reserve lockers from your phone. To avoid an abusive number of reservations, the rules are the following:

  • You can only reserve one compartment at a time.
  • You have 30 minutes after clicking the "Reserve" button to deposit your package into the compartment. After this time, you receive a penalty point.

How does your penalty score work?

If you do not deposit the package in time, your score increases by 1 and your account is locked for a duration based on the following formula:

24h × (penalty score − 1)

The "−1" means that for your first missed reservation you only get a warning and can still reserve compartments.

To bring your penalty score back to 0, you need to deposit a package in time 7 times in a row.
